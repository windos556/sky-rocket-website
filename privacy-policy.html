Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 October 2026

Sky Rocket L.L.C-FZ ("Sky Rocket", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect through our website and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact Sky Rocket, request a quotation, request a service, or submit an account or business enquiry, we may collect information that you provide to us, including:

  • Your name
  • Company or organization name
  • Business email address
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • Job title or business role
  • Business and service requirements
  • Aircraft, flight, routing, airport, fuel, permit, ground-handling, charter, or other operational information that you provide in connection with a request
  • Information contained in your communications with us
  • Other information that you voluntarily provide through our website or directly to Sky Rocket

We only collect information that is reasonably relevant to responding to your enquiry or providing our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests
  • Prepare and provide quotations
  • Arrange requested aviation services
  • Communicate with customers, suppliers, and service providers
  • Coordinate fuel, permits, ground handling, charter, and other aviation services
  • Establish and manage business relationships and accounts
  • Maintain business and operational records
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
  • Protect our website, systems, and business from unauthorized or fraudulent activity

3. Sharing of Information

Where necessary to provide a requested service, Sky Rocket may share relevant business or operational information with third-party aviation service providers involved in fulfilling that request.

Depending on the service, these parties may include:

  • Aviation fuel suppliers and into-plane service providers
  • Flight permit and aviation service providers
  • Ground handling companies
  • Airports, FBOs, and other aviation service providers
  • Charter operators and other service partners

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, regulation, court order, or government authority.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Information You Provide for Aviation Services

When requesting aviation services, you may provide operational information required to arrange the requested service.

This may include information relating to an aircraft, flight, airport, route, fuel requirement, permit requirement, ground handling requirement, charter requirement, or other aviation operation.

Such information is used for the relevant business and operational purpose.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided to us against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

Sky Rocket is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their applicable privacy policies separately.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including applicable business, contractual, accounting, legal, and regulatory requirements.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request information about personal data we hold about you or to request correction of inaccurate information.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this website with a revised effective date.
